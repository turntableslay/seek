Our story

A celebration of flavor, culture and connection

Step inside and the city slips away. Warm light, plush seating and good music set the tone for a long evening, whether it’s a first date, a birthday or a night out with the whole group.

The kitchen cooks Southern and Cajun comfort food with a little polish, and the bar keeps the drinks coming. Out on the patio, the downtown skyline does the rest.
